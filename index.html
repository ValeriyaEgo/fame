About

FAmeStudioAgency is a European-based representation office specializing in premium furniture and lighting for residential and contract projects.
We represent selected manufacturers on the markets of Germany, Austria, and Poland, working closely with architects, interior designers, developers,
and private clients.

With over 20 years of experience in the furniture and lighting industry, we bring deep market knowledge, technical expertise, and a clear understanding of how European manufacturing works
in real projects. Our experience allows us to anticipate challenges, optimize decisions, and deliver reliable results at every stage.

We do not represent factory catalogues- we represent carefully selected manufacturers. Our
partners are chosen for their professionalism, production reliability, consistent quality, and ability to
support projects across different styles and levels of complexity. Each factory we work with is capable of meeting high design, technical, and logistical standards

Francesco Molon (Italy)


Italian Premium Furniture Manufacturer

For decades, Francesco Molon has been synonymous with exceptional craftsmanship, refined materials, and distinctive Italian design. The brand is internationally recognized for its ability to combine tradition with contemporary interpretation, offering collections that range from classic elegance to modern luxury.

Francesco Molon furniture is widely specified by:
• interior designers
• architects and project planners
• private clients seeking bespoke, high-quality interiors

Each collection reflects a unique design language, meticulous attention to detail, and uncompromising standards of production

United Alabaster (Spain)


Spanish Premium Lighting Manufacturer

FAme Studio also represents United Alabaster, a Spanish manufacturer specializing in premium lighting made from natural alabaster.

United Alabaster is known for its sculptural approach to light, where each piece becomes both a
functional object and a design statement.
The use of natural alabaster gives every luminaire a unique character, creating warm, atmospheric lighting with a strong architectural presence.

The brand is highly regarded by designers and architects for:
• its distinctive materiality
• timeless, minimalist aesthetics
• suitability for high-end residential and hospitality projects

How can I get 3D models of specific products?

Complete the registration form on our website, and we’ll send you a download link with the 3D models you need from any of our partner factories.

How can I request a finishes sample box for my project?

Submit the registration form on our website, and we’ll contact you immediately. We guarantee delivery of a sample box within 24 hours.

Yes, we provide full assistance in organizing local events and presentations together with our partner factories, with a strong focus on sales and client engagement.

Yes. We offer a complete range of services — from introducing you to the factory to managing delivery and installation of finished products at your location.
