About FAME STUDIO

Bridging Brands and Professionals

FAME STUDIO is a representative office for furniture and lighting manufacturers, as well as producers of decorative items and interior accessories. We represent the interests of world-renowned factories in promoting their products in selected regional markets.

Our Clients
Interior showrooms, online furniture stores, lighting boutiques, procurement departments, developers, designers, and architects.

Our Role
We serve as the marketing and service department of the factories we represent — providing professionals with access to the latest collections, catalogs, materials, and technical expertise.

Our Promise
All FAME STUDIO services are fully covered by the factories we represent. Our clients purchase directly at factory prices, while benefiting from personalized local support — completely free of charge.
